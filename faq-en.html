Frequently Asked Questions

OFFICIAL FAQ & QUESTIONS DIRECTORY
"GIA VIET IN MY HEART 2026"

A comprehensive guide covering 28 official questions about rules, categories, artwork requirements, video presentation standards, and prize structures. Search by keyword or filter by topic below.

Showing: 28 / 28 questions
# Question from Participants & Parents Answer from the Organizing Committee
#01

Can students enter as a group or team (e.g., pairs or classmates)?
No. Every contestant must register individually.
#02

Which category should my child join based on their current class level?
•Kids Board: Students from First Friends 1A up to Family & Friends 4C.
•Teens + ADU Board: Students from EFT-Foundation 1 up to IELTS course levels.
•Teacher & Staff Board: All current teachers, staff, and teaching assistants.
#03

Can students who recently completed a course or are currently on reserve register?
Yes. Students who completed their latest course within the past 4 months or are waiting for the next term are fully eligible to participate.
#04

Is there an entry or participation fee?
Participation is completely free of charge for all students, teachers, and staff at Gia Viet.
#05

How do I register for the contest?
You may choose either of two methods:
•Online: Fill in the Google Form HERE or scan the QR code on the website.
•In-Person: Register at the reception desk of any of the 4 Gia Viet branches during working hours (7:30 – 11:30; 13:30 – 21:00).
#06

What should I do after completing the registration form?
Join the official contest Zalo group to receive submission guidelines, milestone updates, and direct support from the Organizers.
#07

Can one participant submit multiple creative formats (e.g., both a poster and a poem)?
Each contestant may submit only 01 creative display product (either a poster, a poem, a painting, etc.). Multiple entries in different formats from the same participant are not accepted.
#08

What components must be submitted? Can I submit only a painting without a video?
Both 02 components are mandatory:
1.One creative display product.
2.One English presentation video.
(Missing either component renders the entry invalid).
#09

What types of creative display products are allowed?
A wide variety: paintings, drawings, posters, handwritten cards, short comic books, self-composed poems/songs, 3D craft models from various materials... (illustrative artwork is highly encouraged).
#10

What are the size requirements for the display product? Are A3 or larger 3D models allowed?
The minimum size is equivalent to standard A4 paper (21 × 29.7 cm) or larger. Sizes such as A3, A2, or larger physical models are warmly encouraged. For custom 3D structures, please consult the Organizers in advance.
#11

Can students draw digitally on an iPad/computer and print it in color?
Yes. While traditional handcrafted artwork (hand-drawn, paper crafts) is strongly encouraged, self-designed digital artwork created by the participant on graphic software is accepted (strictly no AI generation).
#12

Can parents or teachers help children make their creative product?
The artwork must be created by the participant. Parents and teachers may guide concepts or help with safety (scissors, glue), but must not make the artwork on the child's behalf, ensuring authentic creativity and honesty.
#13

Will contestants get their physical artwork (painting, model) back after the contest?
Most entries are retained by the Organizers for internal exhibition and gallery displays. If a participant specifically wishes to retrieve their work, please discuss directly with the Organizing Committee.
#14

What are the technical and duration specifications for the presentation video?
•Duration: Under 2 minutes 30 seconds.
•Format: Must be recorded in landscape orientation (16:9), with crisp audio and balanced lighting.
#15

Must the contestant appear on camera, or can they just zoom into the painting with voiceover?
The contestant must visibly appear in the video, presenting directly in English alongside their physical product.
#16

For young Kids learners who are not yet fluent, how will their speaking be graded?
Judges evaluate candidates appropriately based on their age and course level. Young learners simply need to confidently state their name, class, and share a few simple sentences about their drawing. Confidence and effort to speak English are rewarded highly.
#17

Can contestants use cue cards or notes while recording their video?
Holding brief keyword cue cards is permitted, but contestants should avoid reading word-for-word from a script to maintain natural delivery and eye contact.
#18

Can background music, subtitles, or video editing effects be added?
Yes. The scoring rubric allocates 10% for audio, visual clarity, and editing quality. However, background music must not overpower or drown out the speaker's voice.
#19

What specifically is the "No AI" rule?
Generative AI tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, ChatGPT scripts, AI voice generators, etc.) are strictly prohibited. All submitted works and speeches must be originally made by the contestant.
#20

Can contestants include photos of teachers, classmates, or the Gia Viet logo in their artwork?
Highly encouraged! The contest is all about celebrating gratitude, memories, and personal bonds with teachers, peers, and Gia Viet.
#21

Where do I submit the physical product and the video file?
•Physical Product: Submit directly at the reception desk of any Gia Viet campus.
•Video File: Upload via the official Google Form portal HERE.
#22

What is the deadline for submitting entries?
Submissions are received from October 29, 2026 until the final deadline at 23:59 on November 30, 2026.
#23

Does submitting early provide any competitive advantage?
Yes, significantly! Earlier submissions are approved and posted to the Gia Viet Fanpage sooner, giving contestants more time to gather social reactions, comments, and shares for the Most Voted prize.
#24

How are voting interaction points calculated on Facebook?
Points are exclusively tallied on the official post published on Gia Viet's Fanpage:
•1 Like / Reaction = 1 point.
•1 Positive comment = 2 points (each account counted once, spam filtered).
•1 Public share = 3 points.
#25

Do likes and comments on re-shared posts on personal timelines count?
No. The system only counts interactions made directly on the original post on Gia Viet's official Fanpage.
#26

Does the social voting score affect Main Category Prizes (Prize A, B, C)?
No. Main prizes (A, B, C, Consolation) are judged 100% independently by the professional Judge Panel (50% Product + 50% Video). Social voting is evaluated strictly for the separate "Most Voted" auxiliary prize.
#27

How long are the course vouchers valid and can they be transferred to others?
Course vouchers can be used by the contestant or gifted/transferred to friends and family.
Voucher expiration date: December 31, 2027.
#28

If a participant does not win a top prize, do they receive any recognition or souvenir?
Yes, absolutely! Every participant who submits a valid entry receives 01 Official Certificate of Participation and an Exclusive Souvenir Gift from Gia Viet.
