Submission Specifications

What Do Participants Need to Prepare?

A complete contest submission consists of 02 mandatory components: 01 Display Product and 01 English Video Presentation, strictly following the contest guidelines.

1. Display Product

Handcrafted Creative Artwork
Size Standard Minimum equivalent to A4 paper (21 × 29.7 cm) or larger
Diverse formats: Drawings, paintings, posters, poetry, short comic books, handwritten cards, or 3D handcrafted models.
Flexible consultation: Participants are welcome to discuss custom dimensions or unique formats directly with the Organizers.

2. Video Presentation

English Presentation
Duration Standard Maximum duration: 2 minutes 30 seconds
Landscape format (16:9): Mount the camera horizontally, ensure clear video resolution, balanced lighting, and distinct audio.
Live spoken delivery: The contestant speaks directly in English, confidently conveying the meaning of their artwork and their affection for Gia Viet.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy

  • AI-generated works are strictly prohibited: Do not use generative AI tools to draw, write scripts, or generate videos. Any AI use results in immediate disqualification.
  • Authentic craftsmanship encouraged: Hand-drawn, hand-written, physically constructed, or self-designed using graphic software.

Copyright & Ownership Regulations

  • Original work: The entry must be created by the participant; submitters assume full responsibility for original content and copyright. Violations will lead to disqualification.
  • Usage rights: Gia Viet English Center reserves the right to document, publish, and display submitted artworks across media channels and internal exhibitions.

Auxiliary Prize Criteria

Social Media Engagement Scoring

This scoring criterion exclusively applies to the "Most Voted" auxiliary prize. Points are calculated based on genuine reactions (Likes/emojis), positive comments, and public shares directly on the official Facebook Fanpage of Gia Viet English Center.

1 Point
Per Like or Reaction

Each unique user reaction (Like, Love, Care, etc.) on the official contest post.

2 Points
Per Positive Encouraging Comment

Each positive and supportive comment from an authentic account counts as 2 points.

3 Points
Per Public Share

Each public share of the official post to a personal Facebook timeline earns 3 points.

Fairness & Etiquette: The Organizers will moderate and delete any hostile, offensive, or disparaging comments against participants.
Spam / Bot Filtering: Repetitive or bot-driven interactions are filtered out. Each individual user account is counted only once for scoring.
Shared Post Interactions: Reactions on re-shared posts do not count towards the total. Only direct interactions on the official Gia Viet post are tallied.
